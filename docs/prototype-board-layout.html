Board layout: ElectroCookie half-size board

The view is from the top, with letters matching your new board (A…E on the left, F…J on the right). Every hole has a name like E15 (column letter + row number).

How this board works (and why it makes things easier)

Each row of 5 holes, A–E (left) and F–J (right), is already joined together. Anything you put in the same 5-hole strip is connected, so you don't need solder bridges. The long + and − lines down the sides (rails) are also joined along their full length. We use the left − rail for GND and the left + rail for 5V. The right-hand rails aren't used.

The whole board

Light-blue upright = 330 Ω resistor. White line = wire to the − rail. Ringed holes = wires from the hood board.

Step 0: check the fit first

  1. Drop a PC817 across the middle gap. Its legs should go into columns E and F. If they don't quite reach, gently bend the legs outward a little.
  2. Plug the Wi-Fi Mini into its female headers and sit it across the gap with the pins in columns B and I, rows 1–8. Turn it so the row labelled 5V · G · D1 · D2 is on the left (column B) with 5V at row 8.
  3. If either doesn't fit like that, send me a photo and I'll adjust the layout.

Right way round

  1. PC817: the dot goes top-left, in column E. All five face the same way.
  2. Resistors stand upright between two rows in column D (fold one leg back down beside the body). Either way round is fine.
  3. Capacitor: long leg in the + rail, striped leg in the − rail (row 12).
  4. Hood legs A/B can go either way round. If a button doesn't respond, just swap its two wires.

Build order

  1. Solder the female headers in columns B and I, rows 1–8. Keep the Wi-Fi Mini plugged in while you solder so they stay straight, then unplug it.
  2. Solder the 5 PC817s across the gap at rows 15, 18, 21, 24 and 27 (dot top-left).
  3. Solder the 5 upright resistors in column D, from the row above each PC817 down to its top row.
  4. Add the capacitor across the left rails at row 12.
  5. Add the wires: 5 signal wires, 5 short wires from column A to the − rail, plus the G and 5V wires.
  6. Last, solder the hood wires: 10 into column J and the 2 power wires into the left rails at the bottom.

Check before connecting to the hood

  1. Multimeter on continuity: the − rail should beep to the Wi-Fi Mini G pin, and the + rail and − rail must not beep to each other.
  2. Program the Wi-Fi Mini over USB before it goes in the hood. After that, update it over Wi-Fi only, and never plug USB in while it's connected to the hood.
